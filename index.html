Card Spend Analyzer

Upload your SBI savings account statement (PDF). See what you paid to each credit card and your total spend for the month.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

For SBI e-statements this is usually the last 5 digits of your registered mobile number followed by your date of birth (DDMMYY).

Credit card payments

Monthly expense

Total expense = credit card payments + other debits. Rows marked “Exclude” (e.g. transfers to your own accounts, investments) are not counted.

My cards & matching rules

These rules decide which payment belongs to which card. Changes apply to this session only. Click Download config.js and replace js/config.js in your repo to keep them.

Transactions

Change the category on any debit to fix it. Payments made through CRED and similar apps don't say which card was paid, so assign those yourself.
